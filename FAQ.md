# FAQ

## Do I need advanced math before starting?

No. Start with intuition, then learn the math when it explains model behavior.

## Should I learn classical ML before deep learning?

Yes. Classical ML builds evaluation discipline and strong baselines.

## Can I become an LLM engineer without training huge models?

Yes. Many real roles focus on evaluation, RAG, tools, agents, inference, and product integration.

## How should I use notebooks?

Run them, change one thing, predict the result, and explain what changed.

## What makes a project interview-ready?

A clear problem, baseline, metric, error analysis, and production discussion.

## How often should I review?

Use quizzes weekly and mocks during the final six weeks.
